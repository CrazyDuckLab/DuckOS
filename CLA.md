# DuckOS Contributor License Agreement (CLA) — DRAFT

> **STATUS: DRAFT — review before enabling external code contributions.**
>
> This document is a draft. It is **not** represented as
> lawyer-reviewed or legally approved. Nothing in this draft creates a
> binding agreement until both the contributor and the project owner
> sign a finalized CLA based on this text or its successor.
>
> The current draft describes the model the DuckOS project intends to
> use. The final CLA will be published as a separate signable
> artifact. Until then, see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for
> what kinds of contribution are accepted today.

## 1. Purpose

This CLA clarifies the rights a contributor grants to the DuckOS
project owner (the entity that distributes DuckOS Community and
controls the DuckOS trademarks), so that the project can:

- distribute DuckOS Community under GPL-3.0-only;
- also make future contributions available under separate commercial
  licenses when the project owner decides to offer them;
- preserve clear authorship and copyright records for every
  contribution.

The CLA does **not** transfer ownership. The contributor keeps
copyright in their work.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Contribution"** means any code, documentation, configuration, or
  other original work of authorship that the contributor submits to
  the DuckOS project for inclusion, whether through a pull request,
  patch, mailing list, issue attachment, or other accepted channel.
- **"Contributor"** means the individual or legal entity that submits
  the Contribution and that has authority to make the grants in this
  CLA.
- **"Project Owner"** means the entity that distributes DuckOS
  Community and controls the DuckOS marks. Today this is the original
  creator, Viacheslav Fomenko; see [`COPYRIGHT.md`](COPYRIGHT.md).
- **"DuckOS Community"** means the open-source edition distributed
  under the GPL-3.0-only, as defined in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
- **"Effective Date"** means the date the contributor accepts the
  finalized CLA for a given Contribution.

## 3. Copyright license grant

The Contributor grants the Project Owner a **perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable** license to:

- use, reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display,
  publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute the Contribution, in
  whole or in part, in any medium, with or without modifications;
- sublicense the foregoing rights through multiple tiers of
  sublicensees;
- relicense the Contribution, alone or together with other
  contributions, under any license terms chosen by the Project Owner,
  including licenses that are not open source.

This grant is **broad enough** for the Project Owner to:

- continue distributing DuckOS Community under GPL-3.0-only;
- offer the same Contribution, or future versions incorporating it,
  under separate commercial licenses, OEM agreements, or other
  proprietary terms;
- reorganize, rebrand, or repackage the Contribution as part of a
  larger work without asking for further permission from the
  Contributor.

## 4. What the Contributor retains

The Contributor retains:

- copyright and authorship in the Contribution;
- the right to use, modify, distribute, and sublicense the
  Contribution for any purpose, including under terms different from
  DuckOS Community, provided the Contributor's exercise of those
  rights does not conflict with the license already granted to DuckOS
  recipients under GPL-3.0-only for the Contribution as distributed in
  DuckOS Community.

Attribution will be preserved in the project's contribution records
and in any NOTICE or AUTHORS file the project maintains.

## 5. Patent grant

To the extent the Contributor controls patents that would be
infringed by the Contribution as used, modified, or distributed by
the Project Owner under any of the licenses it may grant, the
Contributor grants a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free** patent license to make, use, sell, offer for sale,
import, and otherwise transfer the Contribution.

This patent grant terminates for any Contributor who initiates patent
litigation against the Project Owner or any recipient alleging that
the Contribution infringes a patent, to the extent the litigation is
filed before the Contributor has first given the Project Owner a
reasonable opportunity to address the alleged infringement.

## 6. Representations and warranties

The Contributor represents that:

- the Contributor is the original author of the Contribution, or has
  sufficient rights to submit it under this CLA;
- the Contribution does not, to the Contributor's knowledge, infringe
  the copyright, patent, trademark, or trade secret of any third
  party;
- the Contribution is not subject to any obligation of confidence
  that would prevent its inclusion in DuckOS Community;
- the Contribution is not subject to any incompatible open-source
  license whose terms would conflict with this CLA or with the
  GPL-3.0-only distribution of DuckOS Community.

Except as expressly stated in this section, the Contribution is
provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind.

## 7. Third-party material

If the Contribution includes material owned by a third party, the
Contributor:

- identifies that material and its license in the contribution;
- provides any required notices or attribution;
- confirms that the third-party license is compatible with this CLA
  and with GPL-3.0-only distribution.

## 8. No transfer of marks

This CLA does not grant any right to use the DuckOS name, DuckOS
wordmark, the rubber-duck logo, or other DuckOS product identity.
Trademark use is governed separately by
[`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).

## 9. No support obligation

Nothing in this CLA obligates the Contributor, the Project Owner, or
any recipient to provide support, maintenance, updates, or bug fixes
for the Contribution.

## 10. Effective Date and acceptance

The DRAFT above has no legal effect. The final CLA will:

- identify the Contributor and the Effective Date;
- be accepted by an explicit recorded action (such as clicking
  "I agree" on a signable form, signing a counterpart, or
  contributing under a documented workflow that records acceptance);
- be the only CLA under which subsequent Contributions are accepted
  until superseded.

## 11. Until the CLA is finalized

Until a finalized CLA is published and accepted:

- **Issues, testing reports, and documentation feedback** are
  welcomed without CLA, under the contribution terms in
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **External code contributions that would affect DuckOS
  relicensing** are not accepted. Submitters who want to contribute
  code are encouraged to wait for the finalized CLA, or to discuss
  alternatives on an issue first.

## 12. Why this draft exists

The DuckOS project intends to support both an open-source Community
edition and a future commercial track. Without an explicit grant
covering relicensing, individual contributions could later block the
commercial track or create license uncertainty for downstream users.

This draft spells out the intended model so that:

- contributors understand what they are agreeing to;
- the project has the rights it needs to operate both tracks;
- authorship and copyright stay with the people who actually wrote
  the code.
